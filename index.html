Privacy Policy

Wi-Fi Doctor · last updated November 10, 2026

1. Summary

Wi-Fi Doctor (“the app”) is a network analysis and diagnostics tool published by TeamCoolApps. It analyzes your own Wi-Fi network, runs diagnostics, and measures connection quality.

  • Wi-Fi Doctor has no account, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no server of ours to send anything to.
  • Nothing about your networks — names, MAC addresses, signal readings, device lists — ever leaves your phone.
  • Optional tools you start (speed test, public IP lookup) contact the services they are designed to test.

2. Data we collect

Nothing is collected by us. The app does not transmit personal or device data to the developer or to any third party for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

5. Network activity and third parties

Wi-Fi Doctor makes outbound requests only for features you explicitly start:

  1. Speed test — transfers throwaway bytes to and from Cloudflare’s public measurement endpoint (speed.cloudflare.com). No identifying payload is sent.
  2. Public IP lookup — asks ipinfo.io (falling back to ipify.org) what your public address is. Runs only when you tap Look up.
  3. Ping, traceroute, DNS, port scan, Wake-on-LAN — send packets to the host you enter, and nowhere else.

If you never open the speed test or public IP tool, the app makes no internet requests at all. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Readings are held in memory and discarded when the app is closed. Settings and coverage-survey readings remain on your device until you clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Wi-Fi Doctor → Storage → Clear storage or uninstall the app.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
